IDEAS

The advent of the world wide web has dramatically changed how society communicates ideas. It holds the absolute power of instantaneous information transfer. If it collapsed today, the world would essentially come to a halt. Its capabilities far surpass that of every human on the planet, and nearly every human on the planet is wholly reliant on that power. One could say the web is like a god of sorts. It knows everything about us: how we live, how we think, how we feel. It knows what we did today. It knows what we have done in the past. It knows what we might do tomorrow. It holds our ideas for the future. And for every second that passes, millions upon millions of ideas are shared and sent across the web, waiting for someone to receive them. These ideas shape our very lives- day by day.
